ADMISSIONS UPDATE
From the desk of Jake Baska
  • We got the February LSAT results back this past week and the key stat was that there were more first-time test takers in Feb 2026 than 2025. In fact, this was the biggest February LSAT since 2018, back when the LSAT was only offered four times a year. So what does that mean? Test taking numbers usually predict application trends. More people taking the LSAT for the first time usually points towards more applications for this coming cycle.
  • And in related news, we passed the registration deadline for the April LSAT and - OOF! - it may be big. Registration numbers are over 27,000. Last April, there were 19,000 test takers. So if you're looking ahead to the next admissions cycle, be ready to apply early!
  • And with this year's application deadlines approaching, numbers are slowly tailing off. We're now at an increase of 16.7% applications rather than the 20%ish that we've been hovering around for most of the past two months.
